Frequently asked

How do I share a board with someone?

Open the board, tap the ⋯ menu in the top-right, and pick Share. You'll get an invite link to send via Messages, AirDrop, or anything else. The recipient taps the link and they're added to the board the moment they sign in.

Prefer typing? They can also pick Join with code from the same menu and enter the 10-character code from the link.

An item disappeared. Can I get it back?

Yes — items get archived, not permanently deleted, when you swipe them away. Scroll to the bottom of the board to see the archived (faded, struck-through) section. Swipe right on an archived item and tap the ↶ icon to restore it. It comes back at the bottom of the active list.

Permanent deletion is a separate, confirm-required action available from the trash icon on archived items.

Why does Sign in with Apple ask for permission?

Family Board uses Sign in with Apple as its only login. Your Apple account ID is the only thing stored — no email is shared unless you choose to. Boards and items live in a Supabase database, accessible only to members of each board.

How do I leave a board someone shared with me?

Open the board, tap ⋯, pick Leave board, and confirm. You'll lose access to that board. The owner and other members are unaffected.

If you're the owner, the menu shows Delete board instead — that removes the board and its items for everyone.

Can I reorder items on a board?

Yes. Long-press an item, then drag up or down to a new position. Everyone on the board sees the new order on their next refresh.

Privacy

Family Board doesn't track you, doesn't show ads, and doesn't share data with third parties. The data you put on a board is visible only to members of that board.

Sign in with Apple is the only login. Your Apple ID identifier is stored to associate boards and items with you. If you sign out, you can sign back in with the same Apple ID and reach your boards again.
